Hobbies

Friends & Family

I love to spend time with my friends. We play games together, watch shows, cook, and so much more. Since moving to Duluth away from all of my family they have become very important to me and I now consider them family. My family is relatively small and very close. Pretty much all of them live in the same neighborhood as me so I see them all very often when I am home.

Golfing

I was on the golf team throughout high school and have continued to golf recreationally since I graduated. I love how individual the sport is and also how it is something I can enjoy with loved ones while also being outside. I like to golf with my former teammates and my family, especially my grandma.

Hiking

I so enjoy hiking because it is a way to appreciate nature while also getting active. I love to hike with my friends and family and also alone. It is very peaceful because of the slower pace and is a great time to do some thinking. Duluth is a great area for hikers and I feel very fortunate to be able to do some of the best hikes in Minnesota.

Traveling

Seeing new places has always been one of my favorite things to do. I love to explore and see how other areas differ from my normal. It is so exciting for me to get out of my element and gain new experiences.
